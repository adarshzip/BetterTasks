BetterTasks

A better Google Tasks panel, built for coursework.

BetterTasks replaces the Google Tasks side panel with one designed for students. It uses your existing Google Tasks data, so there is nothing to migrate and everything stays in sync with the official mobile app.

What it does

Subtasks that look like subtasks

A properly indented tree with connector lines, collapsible parents, and a progress count that keeps counting as you finish pieces instead of shrinking.

Grouped by class

Tag a task with a course and it gets a coloured pill, with the colour taken from that course's own calendar events so the panel and your calendar agree. Group by due date or by class, and order your classes however you think about them.

Type it all in one line

math 458 pset 4 fri 5pm 90m sets the class, due date, time, and effort estimate. The same syntax works in the Google Tasks mobile app, so a task captured during a lecture arrives here fully tagged.

Schedule the work, not just the deadline

Give a task an effort estimate and BetterTasks proposes times to do it, avoiding everything already on your calendar and never landing after the due date. Accepting one creates a real calendar event.

Built for keyboards

Navigate, complete, indent, reorder, and snooze without reaching for the mouse. Press ? in the panel for the full list.

Install

Chrome Web Store Edge Add-ons

BetterTasks asks to access your Google Tasks and Calendar because that is the data it displays and edits. There is no account to create, no subscription, and no server: your data never leaves your browser except to Google's own APIs. See the privacy policy and terms of service.

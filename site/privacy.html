BetterTasks Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

BetterTasks is a browser extension that provides an alternative interface for your own Google Tasks and Google Calendar data. It has no server, no database, and no analytics. This policy describes what it accesses and why.

The short version

BetterTasks does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information. All data stays either in your Google account or in your own browser. Every network request the extension makes goes directly to Google's APIs, authenticated as you, to read and write your own data. There are no other recipients, because there is nowhere else for the data to go.

What it accesses, and why

Google Tasks Read and write your task lists and tasks. This is the extension's core function: displaying, creating, editing, completing, and reordering your tasks.
Google Calendar Read your events to identify your recurring classes and their colours, and to find free time when suggesting when to work on a task. Create and remove work-session events on a dedicated calendar named "BetterTasks". No other calendar is modified.
Your email address Read once, so that the extension can name your account when it renews its access token in the background. Without this, Google refuses to refresh silently and you would be asked to sign in every time your browser restarts. The address is stored only in your browser and is never transmitted anywhere except back to Google during sign-in.

What is stored on your device

The extension keeps a small amount of state in your browser's own extension storage:

  • An access token for your Google account, which expires after one hour.
  • Your email address, used as described above.
  • Display preferences: which view is selected, which sections are collapsed, your preferred ordering of classes, and which suggestions you have dismissed.

None of this leaves your device. It is removed when you sign out or uninstall the extension.

Task metadata

Some features, such as class tags, effort estimates, and priorities, are not supported by the Google Tasks API. BetterTasks stores these as a short line of text at the end of the relevant task's own notes field, inside your Google account. This is your data, in your account, visible to you in Google's own apps. It is not sent anywhere else.

What is not collected

BetterTasks does not collect analytics or telemetry, does not use cookies or tracking of any kind, does not read your browsing history or the content of web pages, and does not use any third-party service, processor, or advertising network. No data is used to train any model. Nothing is sold.

Revoking access

Signing out inside the extension revokes its access token with Google and clears everything stored locally. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the extension removes all local data.

Limited use

BetterTasks' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
